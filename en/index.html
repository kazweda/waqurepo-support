💧 Water Quality Reporter

Simple daily chlorine reporting for water facilities

About

Water Quality Reporter is an app for water facility operators to report residual chlorine measurements by email. Enter your reading, tap Send — the report goes straight to your recipients from your own account.

Key Features

📊 Quick Entry

Enter residual chlorine values quickly with built-in validation.

📧 Direct Delivery

Reports are sent directly from your own account — no third-party servers involved.

📝 Send History

View and manage past reports within the app.

🔒 Privacy First

Your data stays on your device. Nothing is stored on external servers by the developer.

Who Is This For?

  • Water facility operators
  • Water quality inspectors
  • Municipal water management staff
  • Water utility technicians
